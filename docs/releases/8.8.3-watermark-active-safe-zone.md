# AXIS 8.8.3 — Watermark + Active Safe Zone

8.8.3 is a focused refinement release. The public product label remains **8.8**.

## Watermark

- Brand opacity is now a true enabled-state range from **1% to 100%**.
- The center AXIS brand and the corner metadata panel no longer share ambiguous vertical geometry.
- `AXIS / RECORD` is a dedicated header with a separator and measured metadata rows below it.
- Long resolved place names may wrap to two lines instead of colliding with the brand row or being reduced to unreadable text.
- Final photo rendering keeps raw latitude/longitude private.
- Each saved record freezes a `locationSnapshot` containing the resolved place and private resolver inputs used at capture time, so later location refreshes do not reinterpret an older capture.
- Existing 8.8 single-owner location behavior remains intact: explicit locate forces fresh high-accuracy acquisition and the canonical precise reverse-geocoder remains the presentation owner.

## Active training safe zone

The accepted active-training card and the `拍摄记录 / 快速记录` dock are deliberately unchanged.

When viewport height becomes tight, the timeline section becomes dock-aware:

- the runtime measures the real dock rectangle and visual viewport;
- unused vertical gap above the timeline may be reclaimed, capped at a small amount;
- any remaining timeline area is constrained to end above the dock with a 14 px safety gap;
- the timeline itself becomes a contained scroll viewport only when needed;
- the section header remains visible while timeline content scrolls;
- orientation change, Safari visual-viewport resize, active-state changes, timeline mutations and dock size changes trigger recalculation.

No fixed `350px`-style compensation is introduced, and the active card/dock geometry is not moved.

## Build guard

`prepare-883-convergence.mjs` runs after the 8.8.2 convergence owners and before hardened packaging. It fails the release if:

- the 1–100 opacity contract is absent;
- a legacy 4–32 / 4–48 opacity clamp survives;
- the per-record location snapshot is absent;
- long-location wrapping is absent;
- the active safe-zone owner or its CSS contract is absent.
