# AXIS 8.9.1 — Quiet Learning

AXIS 8.9.1 is a focused field-polish release on top of 8.9. It keeps the training state, active-control owner, capture flows, timeline ownership, Local Vision v2 and Frontier Vision contracts intact.

## Rest Speak — complete at a glance

The existing between-set rest slot remains the passive entry point. On iPhone-width layouts the timer stays compact and the target phrase receives up to two lines, so the phrase is no longer deliberately forced into a one-line ellipsis. The active-card outer geometry and its training controls remain owned by the existing v87 / 8.8.4 control system.

A phrase tap no longer speaks immediately. It opens a small, non-modal learning panel anchored above the active card. The panel has no backdrop, does not replace the training card, and never autoplays audio. Pronunciation plays only after the explicit “听发音” action.

The learning panel can show:

- complete target phrase and natural meaning;
- scenario, CEFR-style level and register;
- practical pronunciation / connected-speech coaching;
- native-use note;
- pattern, alternative wording and likely response;
- common learner mistake;
- memory anchor;
- IELTS Speaking extension when the phrase is suitable.

“换一句” advances within the current rest exposure. “已掌握” is stored only in the Rest Speak accessory store and de-prioritises that item without writing training metadata.

## English curriculum

The English pool grows from 12 seed phrases to 72 structured units. The added curriculum covers gym interaction, everyday conversation repair, travel/service situations, work/study communication and IELTS-style opinion/discussion language. It is built around usable chunks rather than isolated vocabulary.

The total multilingual bank is 108 units in this release. Japanese, Korean and Chinese retain the existing compact pronunciation-assisted path; the richer IELTS layer is English-specific.

## Detail reveal stability

`训练轨迹 / 记录 -> 单项详情` keeps the 8.9 atomic title/body handoff and adds a stable visual shell:

- old content stays visible while the next detail is prepared;
- an already-open detail sheet is never closed and reopened;
- the current body height is held during an in-place swap;
- the 72 ms delayed history reveal is removed;
- the detail surface is precomposed before it becomes interactive;
- detail-only backdrop blur is retired to avoid Safari compositing flashes.

The first visible frame of a newly opened item must already contain the complete title and detail body.

## Ownership

8.9.1 adds no Rest Speak interval, MutationObserver, ResizeObserver, training-state owner, timeline owner, capture owner or sound owner. The learning panel is user-invoked and closes when the active-rest context is left.

## Release gates

`axis-build.json` adds:

- `restSpeakInlineComplete`
- `restSpeakMicroLearning`
- `restSpeakRichEnglish`
- `restSpeakNoAutoplay`
- `restSpeakSpacedExposure`
- `detailStableReveal`
- `detailStableInPlaceSwap`
- `detailNoBlurFlash`

Chromium and iPhone WebKit run both the inherited 8.9 regression and the dedicated 8.9.1 learning/detail regression.
