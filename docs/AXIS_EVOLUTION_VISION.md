# AXIS Evolution Vision

## Position

AXIS is not designed to become another fitness dashboard, workout logger, AI coach, social content platform, or generic life journal.

The long-term product is a **Personal Evolution Engine**: a system that lets repeated real-world behavior accumulate into private, inspectable personal evolution and eventually into truthful personal works generated by time.

The first domain is training. The underlying model is broader: repeated activities where a person returns, practices, changes, learns, adapts, or recovers.

Examples include strength training, running and walking routes, swimming, climbing, rehabilitation, dance, instrument practice, sport skills and other repeatable physical or practiced activities.

The expansion principle is not “add many categories”. AXIS keeps one sharp model:

**repeated Encounter + real evidence + time + change.**

## Product loop — Capture and Reveal

AXIS needs two core loops.

### Capture / 留下

A real moment leaves evidence with almost no burden.

Possible evidence includes structured data, photo, short video, Live Photo, location context, route, wearable data and manual confirmation.

The user should not feel that they are creating content or maintaining a database. They are simply leaving a trace of something that happened.

Capture should have progressive friction levels:

- Level 0: a factual record alone is enough to form Evolution;
- Level 1: an optional natural moment may be left with one camera action, never as homework;
- Level 2: the system may later surface a rare objectively meaningful moment with restrained language such as `留下这次`, but never require capture.

### Reveal / 发现

Reveal is the long-term reason to return.

The user should open AXIS thinking:

> I want to see what this has become.

Not:

> I need to enter another record.

Time accumulation should progressively reveal something that was not obvious in the individual records.

## Core model

### Encounter

A single truthful interaction with an activity, object, place, or practice.

Examples: one workout session, one run, one climbing attempt, one rehabilitation exercise, one dance practice, one instrument passage.

### Evolution Object

Repeated Encounters with the same real activity gradually form one understandable personal object.

It is not a table of dates and not a generic chart. It should feel like something that has existed through time.

Examples:

- a seated row Evolution;
- a squat Evolution;
- a running-route Evolution;
- a climbing-route Evolution;
- a rehabilitation-movement Evolution;
- a dance-movement Evolution;
- a practiced musical passage Evolution.

### Evidence Bundle

An Encounter may contain structured facts and optional visual/context evidence. Evidence remains attached to the Encounter that produced it.

Media is not a detached gallery item. Time, factual data and visual evidence belong together.

### Replay

Replay is a downstream Reveal generated from accumulated truthful evidence.

Replay is not a video editor and is not the source of truth. It is one way of seeing a real Evolution after the underlying identity and evidence continuity are already credible.

## Unique AXIS outputs

### Evolution Replay

A short time-compressed view of a real Evolution.

Typical duration may be 5, 10 or 15 seconds, but duration is secondary. The sequence should use real first / meaningful middle / latest evidence and preserve dates or factual values when useful.

It should not require BGM, transitions, motivational subtitles, templates or social publishing. The emotional effect should come from the real time difference itself.

### Evolution Portrait

A future static “time specimen” of one Evolution Object: a highly considered visual artifact built from truthful endpoints, span, encounter count, meaningful factual thresholds and real evidence.

It is not a dashboard screenshot or generic chart. The reference quality is closer to a museum specimen, mission artifact or personal career card, while remaining private and factual.

### Reality Timeline

A future spatial/relational view of repeated reality.

A place such as a gym, route, climbing wall or practice space should not become a photo album. It can reveal the user’s relationship with that place through Encounters and Evolution Objects.

## Current implementation state

### 8.13.1 — truthful Encounter foundation

8.13.1 makes sealed Sessions visible as a time field and proves that real activity evidence can be recovered from canonical training metadata without inventing missing time. Same-day sessions remain distinct, sub-minute sessions remain truthful, and `window.__AXIS_EVOLUTION__` exposes a read-only semantic projection of first/latest Encounter, encounter count, span and media evidence.

### 8.14 — first Evolution Objects

8.14 is the first product step where Evolution becomes directly perceivable rather than only a resolver/API concept.

Inside an expanded real Session, a recorded activity becomes a semantic object entry. Tapping it reveals in place how many real Encounters exist, how long the evidence spans, first/latest factual endpoints, existing media evidence and only literal directly comparable change.

The object does not open a dashboard, require a second database, invoke AI, write training state, or claim that a change is good/bad. First-only Encounters stay first-only.

### 8.15 — Media Evidence Layer

8.15 turns already-recorded photos and short videos into **Encounter-bound time evidence**.

The Media Evidence viewer is not a gallery. It groups evidence by the real Encounter, keeps date and factual data attached, allows navigation across media-bearing Encounters, supports explicit short-video playback without autoplay, and can show real earliest/latest endpoint photos side-by-side when both exist.

No-media Evolution remains complete. AXIS does not ask the user to fill the missing visual history.

This stage deliberately uses a metric-agnostic evidence primitive so the same product model can later serve running, climbing, rehabilitation, dance, music and skills without creating separate category products.

### 8.16 — Evolution Replay

8.16 is the next planned stage.

The first Replay should be extremely constrained and truthful:

- generated from one existing Evolution Object;
- real Encounter evidence only;
- real first / selected middle / latest sequence;
- factual date/data overlays only where useful;
- no invented frames or fabricated continuity;
- no BGM dependency, creator template, motivational copy or publishing workflow;
- no Replay when evidence is insufficient for a truthful sequence.

The objective is not “AI video generation”. It is the first strong **Reveal** produced by accumulated reality.

## Recording philosophy

Recording should become progressively lower friction:

Input everything

→ Confirm what changed

→ Confirm important moments

→ Capture only meaningful differences

→ increasingly passive context on native devices.

AXIS should never fabricate precision. If the user did not provide exact information, uncertainty remains uncertainty.

## Identity philosophy

Evolution requires stable identity across repeated reality.

Identity should come from existing canonical IDs where possible, then careful historical compatibility evidence. Display names alone are fallback evidence, not a reason to silently merge unrelated things.

Identity continuity should strengthen the same universal Evolution Object over time. It must not become a separate user-maintained taxonomy product.

## Media philosophy

Photos and videos inside AXIS are **evidence of time**, not social content.

The product should avoid creator workflows:

- no composition pressure;
- no editing pressure;
- no performance pressure;
- no templates requiring effort;
- no publishing obligation;
- no social ranking.

AXIS organizes naturally captured material into private useful Reveals.

The difference is fundamental:

**social platforms ask people to create content; AXIS lets time create the content.**

The user simply lives, trains, practices or recovers.

## Context and future world model

The forward direction is not to add an AI chat box. It is to make AXIS increasingly capable of remembering truthful relationships between the user and repeated real-world things.

A future Encounter can be understood as:

**a person + a repeated Object + a time + a place/context + available evidence.**

On Web, AXIS establishes reliable semantics and local-first evidence. Native iOS can later add richer passive context through Camera, Live Photo, motion, route, Apple Watch, HealthKit, haptics, widgets and device lifecycle.

The system should gradually move away from:

human understands reality → opens form → chooses category → fills fields

and toward:

reality happens → evidence is left → AXIS associates it with a truthful Encounter → the Evolution Object grows → Reveal appears later.

## Web phase

The Web app should prove the product model through:

- reliable session model;
- truthful Encounter semantics;
- Evolution Objects;
- Media Evidence Layer;
- Evolution Viewer;
- truthful Replay substrate;
- low-friction Capture;
- deterministic local-first architecture.

The goal is not to imitate native iOS behavior.

## iOS phase

Native iOS expands the instrument through:

- AXIS Camera;
- Live Photo;
- Apple Watch;
- HealthKit;
- haptics;
- widgets;
- routes/motion;
- background context.

AXIS Camera should eventually feel less like “take a photo” and more like looking at the present with memory of the past. Pointing at a known repeated object/place can reveal previous Encounters without turning the camera into an AR gimmick.

Widgets and Live Activities should prioritize **world updates / Evolution updates**, not generic activity-ring duplication or nagging reminders.

## User range

AXIS should not expand by trying to serve everyone. It should expand around a shared psychology:

- people intentionally getting better at something;
- long-term practitioners;
- rehabilitation users;
- people who value collecting meaningful evidence of their own life;
- coaches/professionals where viewing another person’s Evolution is appropriate and explicitly shared.

Training remains the first proving domain, not the permanent market boundary.

## UX language

AXIS copy should be direct, factual, professional, calm and human.

Avoid tutorials, motivational slogans, AI-assistant language, vague promises and evaluative claims without evidence.

Prefer:

`首次记录：25kg`

`最近一次：40kg`

`共记录 18 次，跨度 76 天`

`最高重量从 60kg 到 85kg`

A literal factual delta may be shown when both endpoints exist. AXIS should not automatically translate it into `变强了`, `进步了` or a score.

## Boundaries

AXIS should not become:

- a social feed;
- a ranking system;
- a generic life journal;
- a task manager;
- a chat-based AI coach;
- a creator/video editor;
- a data-dashboard product.

The product remains focused on one idea:

**Real repeated experiences accumulate into personal Evolution; Reveal makes that accumulated reality worth returning to.**
