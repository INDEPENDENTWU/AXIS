# Code of Conduct

AXIS is maintained as a focused engineering project. Discussion should stay useful to the product and respectful to the people doing the work.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss behavior, evidence, tradeoffs and code rather than attacking people.
- Keep reviews specific enough that another contributor can act on them.
- Assume disagreement is normal; explain the technical or product reason for a position.
- Do not publish another person's private information, media, location, credentials or account data.
- Do not use issues, pull requests or discussions for harassment, discrimination, threats, spam or sexualized attention directed at another participant.

## Project decisions

Maintainers may close, edit, hide or remove contributions that are abusive, unsafe, off-topic, deceptive, disclose private information, or repeatedly ignore the repository's contribution rules.

Technical disagreement is not misconduct. Critical review is welcome when it is about the work and supported by a reproducible case, product contract or engineering argument.

## Reporting

For ordinary contribution problems, use the relevant issue or pull request. For security or privacy-sensitive matters, follow `SECURITY.md` and avoid posting sensitive details publicly.

This policy applies to participation in the AXIS repository and project-managed spaces.
